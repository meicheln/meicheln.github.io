QCM Cyclohexane Sensor

A 10 MHz quartz crystal with a polymer coating, kept oscillating by a Pierce circuit. Vapour that sticks to the coating adds mass, and the frequency drops by 0.2264 Hz for every ng/cm².

  • My own Python solver: 10.001410 MHz, 22.8× start-up margin.
  • It caught the crystal driven at 213 µW. R3 from 1 kΩ to 3.3 kΩ brings it to 93 µW.

1General description

I like projects where a number tells you whether you were right: a crystal's frequency, a backtest's return, an order that did or didn't get recorded.

I'm Meichel, a biomedical engineering student at Institut Teknologi Sumatera (ITERA) in Lampung. My work sits where hardware meets data: I design a circuit, write the software that reads it, then test the result against something that could prove me wrong. The same habit carries over to markets, where I check strategies against chance, and to the refill water depot we run, where orders now arrive through WhatsApp.

2Features

  • Designs analog front-ends and verifies them in simulation before soldering.
  • Builds data tools in Python, with tests for look-ahead leakage and exchange rounding rules.
  • Ships small full-stack apps in React and Node that keep working without an API key.
  • Automates small-business work with WhatsApp and Google Sheets.
  • Publishes the disappointing result next to the good one.

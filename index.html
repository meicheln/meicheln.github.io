QCM Cyclohexane Sensor

A 10 MHz quartz crystal coated with a non-polar polymer, kept oscillating by a Pierce circuit. Cyclohexane sticks to the coating, adds mass, and the frequency drops. An Arduino Due counts it.

  • Schematic in EasyEDA Pro around an SN74LVC1GX04, design-rule check clean.
  • The simulator had no crystal model, so I wrote a solver in Python: it oscillates at 10.001410 MHz with a 22.8× start-up margin.
  • It also showed the crystal being driven at 213 µW, too hard for a coated crystal. Raising R3 from 1 kΩ to 3.3 kΩ brings it to 93 µW.

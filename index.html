QCM Cyclohexane Sensor

A 10 MHz quartz crystal with a polymer coating, kept oscillating by a Pierce circuit. Cyclohexane vapour adds mass to the coating and the frequency drops by 0.2264 Hz for every ng/cm², matching the Sauerbrey equation.

  • The simulator had no crystal model, so I wrote a solver in Python. It oscillates at 10.001410 MHz with a 22.8× start-up margin.
  • It showed the crystal driven at 213 µW, too hard for a coating. Raising R3 from 1 kΩ to 3.3 kΩ brings it to 93 µW.

StockSense

A self-hosted scanner for the Indonesia Stock Exchange that reports how often it is wrong. A naive entry rule lost 96.3% over 3,572 backtested trades, and the app shows that result instead of hiding it.

  • 55 liquid IDX stocks, technical analysis, five separately audited ML models, foreign-flow tracking.
  • Every number comes with its measurement period and a confidence interval.

Depot Air WA

“3 galon” is all a customer of the water refill depot has to type on WhatsApp. The app records the order and replies to confirm it.

  • A local dashboard marks each order new, delivered or cancelled.
  • Orders reach a Google Sheet daily report through an outbox that retries every minute.

Trading Research Lab

Before trusting an idea with money, I check whether it beats a coin flip. 469 trades from my indicator, across 8 pair and timeframe sets, compared with a random-walk baseline.

  • SNIPER SMC, a Pine Script indicator that logs the result of every signal.
  • Python backtesters, MT5 Expert Advisors, and an MCP server that feeds Claude live market data.

I like projects where a number tells you whether you were right: a crystal's frequency, a backtest's return, an order that did or didn't get recorded. I'd rather show a bad number I can explain than a good one I can't.
